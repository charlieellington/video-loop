# Intake — the first conversation about a new video

Plain English: before anything is cut, the agent and the person making the video agree what the
video is, what material exists, and the few choices only they can make. Ask everything in ONE
message, offer a recommendation for each, and record the answers in `project.toml`.

## 1. Look before you ask
- `video-loop doctor <project>` — fix anything marked FAIL first.
- `video-loop ingest <project>` — inventories `media/`, checks the narration, makes contact sheets
  (`.video-loop/cache/thumbs/`) and, unless cleanup is pending, the transcript (`output/transcript.txt`).
- Open the contact sheets (they are images — look at them). Note low-resolution clips, sideways
  clips, clips with useful or distracting sound, and clips that show people.

## 2. Ask (one message, recommendations included)
1. **What is the video?** One sentence: who it is for and what they should feel or do at the end.
2. **Narration:** is the recording final? If it has background noise (hum, traffic, room echo,
   kitchen noise), offer ElevenLabs voice cleanup: it sends ONLY the narration recording to
   ElevenLabs, keeps the original, and lets them compare both by ear before anything is cut.
   (`voice_cleanup.source = "elevenlabs"`; it costs ElevenLabs credits.)
3. **Captions:** show both looks — `outline-phrase` (white words, dark outline) and
   `typewriter-highlight` (typewriter words appear as spoken, current word inverted). Render a
   sample by running `video-loop board` once with each, or show `output/<rev>/caption-sample.jpg`.
4. **Music:** none, their own track (`music.source = "file"`), or generated by ElevenLabs from a
   short brief (`music.source = "elevenlabs"`, `music.prompt`). Propose a brief: mood,
   instruments, tempo, "no vocals, no drums" if it sits under speech. Where: continuous bed
   (`through`) or only at moments.
5. **Where it will be posted** (canvas and length) and **how it should end** (a line, a question,
   a call to action).
6. **Low-resolution or missing material:** say what the footage can and cannot do. Never demand
   new footage; offer holds, stills or reuse.

## 3. Record
Write the answers into `project.toml` (see docs/configuration.md). Then continue with
`prompts/editorial-plan.md`. If cleanup was chosen: `video-loop voice clean <project>`, ask them
to listen to `output/voice-compare.html`, then `video-loop voice select <project> original|cleaned`
and `video-loop ingest <project>` again.
